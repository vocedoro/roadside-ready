Winter Car Emergency Kit Checklist: What to Keep in Your Trunk

Updated November 9, 2026 · Product details checked against manufacturer listings on Amazon on this date

Most winter roadside trouble is boring: a dead battery, an iced-over windshield, a tire low on air, or getting stuck on a snowy shoulder. A good winter kit covers those four problems first, then keeps you warm while you wait. Here is the checklist, what you probably already own, and what is worth buying.

The checklist

  • ☐ Snow brush and ice scraper (long enough for your roof)
  • ☐ Jump starter (or jumper cables plus a friend)
  • ☐ Tire inflator and a tire plug kit
  • ☐ Traction aid: chains or cables if your area allows them, or a bag of sand / non-clumping kitty litter
  • ☐ Warmth: emergency blankets, gloves, hat, extra socks
  • ☐ Phone charging cable or power bank
  • ☐ Flashlight or headlamp with spare batteries
  • ☐ Water and a few non-perishable snacks
  • ☐ Small shovel
  • ☐ Basic first aid kit
Habits matter as much as gear: keep your gas tank at least half full in winter, and check tire pressure and battery health before the first deep freeze.

FAQ

Do lithium jump starters work in freezing weather?

Cold reduces battery output. Check the operating temperature range in the manual, keep the pack charged, and in extreme cold store it indoors overnight and bring it with you.
